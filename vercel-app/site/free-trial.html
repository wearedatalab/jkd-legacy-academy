Questions

Before your first class.

Do I need any martial arts experience?

No. Many students start with no background at all. Your first class is adapted to your level, and the instructor corrects you personally.

Is there a minimum age?

Our classes are for adults, 18 and over.

What should I bring?

Comfortable training clothes (a t-shirt and track pants), indoor shoes or bare feet, and a bottle of water.

How much does it cost after the free class?

The first class is free. After that you can choose a 5-session pack (A$175), a 12-session pack (A$350), a monthly unlimited membership (A$240) or a 3-month unlimited membership (A$650).

Where do I park?

There is free street parking on Hampstead Rd and the surrounding streets in Maidstone.

What happens in the first class?

You warm up with the group, learn the Jeet Kune Do stance, footwork and straight lead punch, and practise simple partner drills. The instructor checks in with you throughout.
