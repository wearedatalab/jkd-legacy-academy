What you’ll try

Four stations. Two hours. One legacy.

Hands-on from the first minute. Small groups rotate through the four pillars of Bruce Lee’s art, coached by Sigung Vargas and our senior students.

Station 01

The wooden dummy

Put your hands on the mook jong, the training partner Bruce Lee practised on for years, and feel how JKD builds structure and timing.

Station 02

The one-inch punch

Learn the mechanics behind the most famous punch in martial arts history: power from the ground, the hips and a relaxed arm.

Station 03

Trapping hands

Pin, slap and hit in one motion. Trapping is JKD's close-range language, and the drill most beginners fall in love with.

Station 04

JKD kicking

The longest weapon to the nearest target. Try the low side kick and the stop kick that intercepts an attack before it lands.

FAQ

Before you come

Is the Bruce Lee birthday event really free?

Yes. The Dragon’s Birthday Experience on Saturday 28 November 2026 is free, including the four training stations, the birthday tribute, cake and refreshments. Spots are limited, so please register so we can plan the room.

Do I need martial arts experience?

No. The morning is designed for complete beginners. Every station starts from zero and our students help you through each drill. If you already train in another style, you are very welcome too.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothes you can move in, like a t-shirt and track pants or shorts. We train barefoot on mats, so no special shoes are needed. Bring a water bottle.

Is it safe?

Yes. There is no sparring. Every drill is supervised by Sigung Ricardo Vargas and senior students, partners work at a controlled pace, and the one-inch punch is practised on pads.

Can I bring a friend?

Absolutely, that is the spirit of a birthday. Tell us how many friends are coming in the form, or ask them to register their own spot.

Who can join?

Teens from 14 and adults of any fitness level. Teens under 18 should come with a parent or guardian, who is welcome to join in.

Where is it and where do I park?

At our academy, Unit 6 / 72-80 Hampstead Rd, Maidstone VIC 3012, in Melbourne’s inner west. There is free parking on site and bus 406 stops nearby. Doors open at 9:00 am.

Why celebrate on the 28th?

Bruce Lee was born on 27 November 1940 in San Francisco. His birthday falls on a Friday this year, so we celebrate the next morning, Saturday 28 November, when everyone can come.
