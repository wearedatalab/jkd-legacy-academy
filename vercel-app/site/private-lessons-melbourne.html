Who it's for

Is a private lesson right for you?

Busy schedules

Shift workers, parents and professionals who can't make the group timetable train at a time agreed with Sigung Vargas.

A specific goal

Self-defence for a particular situation, confidence after a bad experience, or getting fit through martial arts — the session is built around it.

Faster progress

Beginners who want a head start before joining the group, and experienced martial artists who want to go deep into Jeet Kune Do (the Mastery pathway).

How it works

What a private lesson looks like

Each private lesson is 60 minutes with Sigung Ricardo Vargas at the Maidstone school. The first call is about you: your experience, your goals and any injuries, so the session can be planned properly.

Sessions combine technique, pad work and drills with the principles behind them — and, for advanced students, the philosophical mentorship of the Mastery pathway.

What to wear: sports clothes and sports shoes. Price: it depends on your goals and how often you train, so Sigung Vargas confirms it on the call.

FAQ

Questions people ask

How long is a private lesson?

Each private lesson lasts 60 minutes.

How much does a private lesson cost?

The price depends on your goals and how often you train, so it is confirmed on a short call with Sigung Vargas. Group class prices are published on The Way.

Who teaches private lessons?

Sigung Ricardo Vargas, head instructor and founder of Jeet Kune Do Legacy Academy, a second-generation instructor certified by Sifu Jerry Poteet and Sigung Richard Bustillo.

What should I wear?

Sports clothes and sports shoes for private lessons.

Can I combine private lessons with group classes?

Yes. Many students use private lessons to speed up their progress in the group classes.
