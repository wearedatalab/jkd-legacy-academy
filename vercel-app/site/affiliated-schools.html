Jeet Kune Do schools in Australia.

Certified instructors in Bruce Lee's lineage, from Melbourne to Adelaide. Each affiliated school runs independently — contact the instructor nearest you.

Quick answer

Jeet Kune Do Legacy Academy's own school is in Maidstone, Melbourne, led by Sigung Ricardo Vargas. Certified instructors in the same lineage run independent, affiliated schools in St Albans and Templestowe (Victoria) and Adelaide (South Australia), each with its own timetable and prices.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Where can I learn Jeet Kune Do in Melbourne?

At Jeet Kune Do Legacy Academy in Maidstone (inner west) with Sigung Ricardo Vargas, or with affiliated instructors in St Albans (Sifu Thomas Pham) and Templestowe (Sifu Mattia Riccardi).

Is there Jeet Kune Do in Adelaide?

Yes. Sifu Peter Pitrakkos teaches in Adelaide — contact him on 0411 268 793.

Are affiliated schools run by JKD Legacy?

No. They are independent schools led by certified instructors in the same lineage, each with its own timetable and prices.

Do you run seminars in other cities?

Occasionally, including in Sydney. Call 0459 785 073 to ask about upcoming seminars.
