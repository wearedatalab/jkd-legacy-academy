What you learn

What our self-defence classes cover

Self-defence starts long before anything physical happens. Every skill below is taught step by step and adapted to your level.

01 · Awareness

Avoid it before it starts

Reading people and places, keeping your distance, using your voice and knowing when to walk away. The best fight is the one that never happens.

02 · Distance

Stance, footwork and range

A balanced guard and mobile footwork let you control the space between you and a threat — the foundation of Jeet Kune Do's idea of interception.

03 · Striking

Simple, direct strikes

The straight lead, low kicks and basic combinations: few techniques, drilled until they work under stress, not a catalogue of moves.

04 · Close range

Grabs, clinch and getting up

Trapping, clinch work and grappling basics so you can break free from a hold, get back to your feet and create space to escape.

05 · Pressure

Skills that hold up under stress

Partner drills get more realistic as you progress. Sparring starts only at intermediate level, always supervised — and it is never compulsory.

06 · Mindset

Confidence and control

Training teaches calm under pressure and the judgement to use only the force a situation needs. Strength and character go together.

Why Jeet Kune Do

Why Jeet Kune Do works for self-defence

Bruce Lee built Jeet Kune Do — "the way of the intercepting fist" — to be simple, direct and efficient. Instead of fixed forms, you learn principles: intercept the attack, use the shortest line, and adapt to whatever is in front of you.

That makes it a practical choice for self-defence. You train kicking, punching, trapping and grappling ranges in one system, so you are not lost when a situation moves from one range to another.

At our school you learn it from Sigung Ricardo Vargas, who was trained and certified by Sifu Jerry Poteet and Sigung Richard Bustillo — two of Bruce Lee's own students. Read more: Is Jeet Kune Do effective in a real fight?

FAQ

Questions people ask

Do I need to be fit to start self-defence classes?

No. You start at your own level and the instructor scales every drill. Fitness comes from training — you don't need it before your first class.

Is sparring compulsory?

No. Sparring is only introduced at intermediate and advanced level, always under the instructor’s supervision, and you can choose not to spar.

What is the minimum age?

Classes are for teens and adults from 14 years old. We do not run kids’ classes.

What should I wear?

Comfortable sports clothes. Group classes train barefoot. Bring a bottle of water.

How much do self-defence classes cost?

Your first class is free. After that, options start at A$175 for 5 classes, with a 12-class pack (A$350), Monthly Unlimited (A$240) and 3-Month Unlimited (A$650). There is an enrolment fee and no lock-in contract. Details on The Way.
